### Task 8: Ulti rules engine

**Files:** `src/engine/types.ts`, `ranks.ts`, `bids.ts`, `deal.ts`, `play.ts`, `scoreDeal.ts` + tests. Source: the Hungarian Wikipedia "Ulti" article; the values below are binding.

**Interfaces — Produces:**
- Players 0 (human), 1, 2; dealer rotates; 10 cards each + 2 talon. The talon (2 cards) passes through the bidding as described below; the final declarer holds 12, discards 2 face down.
- Ranking: trump games and passz/40-100/20-100/ulti/durchmars: `asz > X > kiraly > felso > also > IX > VIII > VII`; colourless (betli, színtelen durchmars): `asz > kiraly > felso > also > X > IX > VIII > VII`.
- Bids (base value per opponent): Passz 1, Negyvenszáz 4, Ulti 4, Betli 5, Durchmars 6, Húszszáz 8, Színtelen durchmars 12; **piros** (hearts trump) doubles trump bids (Passz, Negyvenszáz, Húszszáz, Ulti, Durchmars) — not Betli or Színtelen durchmars. Combinations allowed: Ulti + Passz / Negyvenszáz / Húszszáz / Durchmars (values add). Bidding: the player after the dealer (forehand) takes the talon first and must bid (at least Passz), discarding 2 cards. In turn, each next player either passes or picks up the 2 discarded cards ("felveszi a talont"), makes a strictly higher bid (equal value: piros beats non-piros) and discards 2. Bidding ends when both other players pass after the last bid; the last bidder is the declarer.
- Play: must follow suit; if following, must beat the trick's current highest card when able; if void, must trump when able (and overtrump when able); otherwise any card. Ulti: declarer must keep the trump VII for the last trick and win it with it.
- Parti scoring: ász and X are 10 each, last trick 10 (total 90); 20/40 announcements (K+F same suit; 40 in trump) count for the announcer's side; declarer wins Passz with more than the defenders. Negyvenszáz/Húszszáz: 100 points with the 40/20.
- Kontra: either defender may double the bid once after the talon is resolved and before the first lead.
- `scoreDeal(result): [number, number, number]` per-player units (declarer ±value×2, each defender ∓value), including piros and kontra multipliers.
- [ ] **Step 1: Failing tests:** both rank orders; legal-play cases (follow, must beat, void→must trump, overtrump, void+no trump→any) including the Review Focus case; bid ordering incl. piros tie-break and combinations; durchmars succeeds only with all 10 tricks; betli fails on the first trick won; ulti fails if the VII is not the winning card of the last trick; parti count with 20/40; scoreDeal table for piros ulti + kontra.
- [ ] RED → GREEN → check. **Commit** 11-13 10:00 `feat(engine): ranks, bids and legal play`; 11-13 12:30 `feat(engine): deal scoring with piros and kontra`.
